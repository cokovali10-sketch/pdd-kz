# Sign and marking attribution

All road sign and marking SVGs in this project are **original geometric drawings** created specifically for JolBilet. They are intentionally simplified versions designed to be legible on dark backgrounds and are not official artwork.

## Source

The sign definitions (codes, names, descriptions in ru/kk/en) in `content/signs.json` are based on the **Rules of the Road of the Republic of Kazakhstan** (ПДД РК), Chapter 8 "Road Signs" (Дорожные знаки).

Sign codes and groups follow the Kazakhstan traffic sign classification:
- **Warning signs (1.x)** — triangular, red border
- **Priority signs (2.x)** — various shapes (diamond, triangle-down, octagon, circle)
- **Prohibitory signs (3.x)** — round, red border
- **Mandatory signs (4.x)** — round, blue
- **Special regulation signs (5.x)** — rectangular, blue
- **Information signs (6.x)** — rectangular, blue
- **Service signs (7.x)** — rectangular, blue
- **Additional information signs (8.x)** — rectangular plates
